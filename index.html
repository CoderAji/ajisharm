PONDER…

nature's system and ours.

  1. why do humans cooperate?

    it's easy. more people = more food = better for me. our brains have evolved to be social creatures because of this, and similar to many other organisms we operate (or try to) and stay in packs.

  2. how did language evolve?

    geographical barriers largely, as well as warfare, have either stopped language from spreading or helped spread it. since the romans loved to stab heads, latin spread everywhere to eventually create the romance languages. now it's usually via the internet, as people spread the message with each other.

  3. why do plants and animals evolve so differently?

    there's no set way to get the best food. some make it themselves while we gather it. additionally, we have a bunch of different organelles in our cells and they do the same. one can't exist without the other.

  4. entropy…?

    entropy measures how random and disordered anything is. nature wants disorder, and it first does that by creating order. while human cells are ordered, with structured body parts and organs, we ourselves are random. one day we might stomp on an ant, the other we might jump off a plane. by creating more ordered cells, eventually nature creates more disorder than if it just kept random atoms floating around.
