WHY I BUILD

Not a biography. The actual reason.

Building is the fastest way I know to find out whether an idea is real. You can argue about a concept forever; a working thing settles it in a week. Education is where I point that instinct, because it's the one lever that compounds — improve how somebody learns and you improve everything they do afterward. Science is the same habit aimed at the world instead of a product: break it down, test it, keep what survives.

Long term, I want to build things that make good learning cheap and ordinary instead of scarce and expensive.
